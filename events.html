Bring us to you

We will run a free Narcan presentation and training for any business, church, school, civic club or neighborhood group in Flagler County, on a date that suits you. Everyone leaves with a kit.

Interested in running a fundraiser?

A restaurant night, a car wash, a raffle at your shop — small local fundraisers are a real part of how this gets paid for, and they take the pressure off a very small team. Tell us what you have in mind and we will help you set it up and turn people out for it.

Need help now?

  • 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Call or text, 24/7.
  • 1-800-662-4357 — SAMHSA National Helpline. Free, confidential treatment referrals, 24/7.
  • 211 — Florida 211. Local treatment, housing, and food assistance.
  • If someone is overdosing right now, call 911. Florida's Good Samaritan law protects people who call for help.

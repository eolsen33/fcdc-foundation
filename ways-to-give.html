Give online

Make a gift now.

Choose a one-time gift or a monthly one. Monthly is more useful to us than the same money given once — it is what lets us say yes to a request in the middle of the year.

Why our donation form has no platform fee

We use Zeffy, which charges nonprofits nothing — no platform fee and no card processing fee. Zeffy funds itself with an optional tip that donors can adjust or decline at checkout. If you would rather all of your gift reach the Foundation, set the tip to zero; nothing is deducted from your donation either way.

Need help now?

  • 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Call or text, 24/7.
  • 1-800-662-4357 — SAMHSA National Helpline. Free, confidential treatment referrals, 24/7.
  • 211 — Florida 211. Local treatment, housing, and food assistance.
  • If someone is overdosing right now, call 911. Florida's Good Samaritan law protects people who call for help.
